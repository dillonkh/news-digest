The reignited US–Iran war over the Strait of Hormuz dominated a turbulent week, as US forces struck roughly 140 Iranian targets in a single round and Tehran declared the waterway closed — the same week the sudden death of Sen. Lindsey Graham reshaped the Senate, June inflation cooled sharply, and two deadly ICE shootings forced a nationwide pause on most vehicle stops. In tech, China's Moonshot AI shipped Kimi K3, the largest open-weight model to date, while a dozen states sued to block the $110 billion Paramount–Warner Bros. Discovery merger and Christopher Nolan's "The Odyssey" opened to a franchise-best $124.5 million.

🏛️ US Politics & Policy

US and Iran lurch back toward all-out war over the Strait of Hormuz

US Central Command carried out consecutive nights of strikes, hitting about 140 Iranian military targets in one round after the IRGC attacked shipping and declared the strait closed. Trump said the US was "taking over the strait" and floated a 20% toll for safe passage. By July 18 an Iranian drone had struck a cargo ship a week after a preliminary deal, with Washington citing the deaths of two US servicemen in Jordan and the Gulf.

CNN, NPR, PBS NewsHour

Sen. Lindsey Graham dies at 71

Graham (R-SC) died the night of July 11 after what his office called a "brief and sudden illness," hours after returning from a trip to Ukraine; the DC medical examiner's preliminary finding was aortic dissection. The last surviving member of an influential group of Senate defense hawks and a close Trump ally, his seat was filled by Gov. Henry McMaster's appointment of Graham's sister Darline, who announced a run for the full term.

NBC News, Washington Post, NPR, Axios

June inflation cools sharply

CPI fell 0.4% in June — the largest one-month drop since April 2020 — bringing annual inflation to 3.5%, down from 4.2% in May and below the 3.8% Wall Street expected, driven by a 5.7% drop in energy prices. Core CPI was flat month-over-month at 2.6% annually. The softer print pushed the odds of a Fed hold at its July 29 meeting to roughly 86% on CME FedWatch.

Bureau of Labor Statistics, CNBC, CBS News

Two deadly ICE shootings force a pause on most vehicle stops

DHS directed ICE to suspend non-urgent vehicle stops after officers fatally shot two people in separate incidents. A newly sworn officer killed 26-year-old Colombian national Johan Sebastián Guerrero during a July 13 stop in Biddeford, Maine, followed by a fatal Houston shooting. Sen. Susan Collins said she urged DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin to halt the stops; border czar Tom Homan estimated the pause would last "a couple of weeks."

NPR, Maine Public, ABC News, CNN

Trump delivers primetime election-security address

In a July 16 East Room speech, Trump alleged "shocking vulnerabilities" in election infrastructure and released declassified documents while pushing the stalled SAVE America Act. Election experts and fact-checkers said the documents contained little new information and no evidence of fraud affecting past counts — his own 2020-era CISA had called that election the most secure in American history.

NPR, Washington Post, CNN, FactCheck.org

House passes State appropriations and the Common Cents Act

The House passed H.R. 8595, providing $47.3 billion for FY2027 State Department and foreign-affairs activities — a $2.7 billion cut from 2026. During the week it also passed the Common Cents Act (H.R. 3074) to stop the Treasury minting the penny and round cash transactions to the nearest five cents.

seMissourian / Targeted News Service

🌍 World News

Record heat wave drives wildfires across Europe

Western Europe endured its third major heat wave of the summer, fueling wildfires in France, Spain, Portugal, and Greece. Around 10,000 people were evacuated in France's Pyrénées-Orientales, and the European Commission said 777 firefighters from 14 countries were deployed. Scientists with World Weather Attribution linked the preceding June heat wave to thousands of excess deaths.

Inside Climate News, Democracy Now!, Insurance Journal

Cuban dissident artist released to Miami

Luis Manuel Otero Alcántara, a prominent Cuban dissident artist and musician, arrived in Miami on July 18 after being released from a five-year prison sentence on the condition that he leave Cuba.

NPR, AP

US–China tech race intensifies

American-led export restrictions continued to block China from some of the world's most advanced technologies, spurring domestic Chinese development and sharpening the rivalry between the two largest economies — a backdrop underscored days later by China's Kimi K3 release.

NPR

💻 Tech News

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 launches as the largest open-weight model

Beijing-based Moonshot AI released Kimi K3 on July 16, a 2.8-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with a 1-million-token context window — the first "open 3T-class model." It debuted #1 on LMArena's Frontend Code Arena at 1,679 Elo, ahead of Claude Fable 5, and scored 57 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, ahead of Claude Opus 4.8 (56) but behind Fable 5 (60). Open weights are due July 27 at $3/$15 per million input/output tokens; rival Chinese AI stocks Z.ai and MiniMax fell sharply.

TechCrunch, Fortune, Tom's Hardware, Simon Willison

PostgreSQL 19 Beta 2 released

The PostgreSQL Global Development Group released 19 Beta 2 on July 16, previewing native SQL/PGQ property-graph queries (ISO SQL:2023), a unified REPACK command with a CONCURRENTLY option for near-zero-downtime reorganization, and temporal FOR PORTION OF syntax. As a pre-release beta, the project expects one or more release candidates before a final release around September/October 2026.

PostgreSQL.org, Linux Compatible

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 and GPT-Live roll out broadly

OpenAI publicly released its GPT-5.6 family (Sol, Terra, Luna) after a government-review-limited preview restricted to roughly 20 organizations, and introduced GPT-Live voice models that listen and speak simultaneously. The broad rollout continued into the digest week.

CNBC, ThursdAI

🐗 Arkansas

Arkansas ranked #1 in the nation for religious liberty

On July 14, Gov. Sarah Huckabee Sanders touted a First Liberty Institute report ranking Arkansas first in religious liberty, up from 27th four years ago. The advocacy-group ranking, which took the top spot from Florida, credited laws including Act 677 of 2025.

Arkansas Democrat-Gazette, Northwest Arkansas Democrat-Gazette

State announces record tourism year

On July 16, Sanders announced Arkansas welcomed a record 54.3 million visitors in 2025, generating $10.2 billion in visitor spending and $17.4 billion in total economic impact, per a Tourism Economics report.

Arkansas Governor's Office

📚 This Week in Arkansas History

On July 17, 1946, the first test run took place at the Atkins Pickle Company plant in Atkins, Arkansas. The factory opened days later with about 100 employees and two buildings housing 57 wooden tanks — each holding 1,000 bushels — and made the town synonymous with pickles, a legacy it still celebrates with an annual Picklefest.

🎬 Pop Culture

"The Odyssey" opens to a franchise-best debut

Christopher Nolan's adaptation of Homer's epic, starring Matt Damon, opened July 17 to $124.5 million domestically and $264.1 million worldwide — Nolan's biggest global opening ever, ahead of "The Dark Knight Rises," "The Dark Knight," and "Oppenheimer." IMAX drove about $51.8 million (23%) of global sales; it earned an "A" CinemaScore, 95% on Rotten Tomatoes, and Universal's biggest R-rated debut behind only the three "Deadpool" films.

Variety, Deadline, Forbes, Hollywood Reporter

Twelve states sue to block Paramount–Warner Bros. Discovery merger

On July 13, a coalition of 12 state attorneys general led by California's Rob Bonta sued under Clayton Act Section 7 to block Paramount Skydance's roughly $110 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery, despite DOJ clearance. The states argued the combined company would control more than a third of blockbuster theatrical distribution and 27% of basic cable; a federal judge paused the deal on July 20.

Axios, CNBC, Deadline, Hollywood Reporter

Live-action "Moana" tops the prior weekend

For the weekend ending July 12, Disney's live-action "Moana" opened at #1 with $43.0 million, more than double the second-place film.
